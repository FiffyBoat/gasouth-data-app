## Module 1: Getting Started on Your Phone or Laptop

### 1.1 Requirements
* Any Android phone, iPhone, iPad, tablet, or laptop.
* Modern web browser (**Google Chrome** or **Safari** recommended).
* Device Location / GPS must be turned **ON** in your phone settings.
* Mobile data connection (MTN, Telecel, AT) or Wi-Fi.

### 1.2 Accessing the App
1. Open your phone's browser and go to:  
   👉 **`https://gasouth-data-app.onrender.com`**
2. **Add to Home Screen (Recommended for Fast Daily Use)**:
   * **On Android (Chrome)**: Tap the 3 dots in the top right corner $\rightarrow$ tap **"Add to Home screen"** or **"Install App"**.
   * **On iPhone (Safari)**: Tap the Share button at the bottom $\rightarrow$ scroll down and tap **"Add to Home Screen"**.
   * An official Ga South DCS icon will appear on your phone screen just like WhatsApp or Facebook!

### 1.3 Logging In
1. Enter your **Username** (e.g. `collector1`, `danny`, `comfort`, `enoch`, `ruth`, etc.).
2. Enter your **Password**.
3. Tap **"Sign In"**.

```

|                                          |
|  Username: [ danny                     ] |
```

## Module 2: Navigating the App & Understanding Your Dashboard

Once logged in, you will see your main navigation menu:

* 📊 **Dashboard**: Summary of your daily target, total records in your zone, records completed, and pending drafts.
* 📝 **Records Register**: The master list of all properties and businesses in your assigned municipal zone.
* 🗺️ **Map Explorer**: Visual spatial map showing all properties as pins across Ga South.
* ➕ **New Capture**: Button to add a brand-new property or shop not already on the municipal register.

### Understanding Record Statuses:
Every record in the system displays one of three status badges:
* 🟡 **DRAFT (Pending GPS Capture / Incomplete)**: Imported from the municipal bill register. Requires a field officer to physically visit the site, lock GPS coordinates, snap photos, and update owner details.
* 🔵 **SUBMITTED (Under Review)**: Completed and submitted by the field collector. Currently waiting for supervisor audit and approval.
* 🟢 **APPROVED (Finalized)**: Verified by the supervisor. Fully active in the official Ga South billing database.

## Module 3: Finding & Opening Assigned Records

In most cases, the properties you need to visit are already in the system as **Drafts**.

1. Tap **"Records"** on the menu.
2. In the search box, you can search by:
   * **Bill Number** (e.g., `BOP-2026-0042` or `PROP-104`)
   * **Ratepayer / Owner Name** (e.g., `Kwame Mensah` or `Grace Enterprise`)
   * **Location / Suburb** (e.g., `Amanfrom`, `Tuba`, `Galilea`, `Bortianor`)
   * **Filter by Status**: Tap **"Draft"** to see only properties needing your attention.
3. Tap on the record card to open the form.

## Module 4: Capturing & Locking GPS Coordinates (Step-by-Step)

The GPS capture card is at the top of the form. Every property must have verified spatial proof.

### The Golden Field Rules:
> [!IMPORTANT]
> 1. **Stand Outside**: Always stand directly in front of the main entrance gate or building door. Never capture coordinates from inside a room or under thick concrete roofs.
> 2. **Clear Sky View**: Keep your phone elevated and step away from tall trees or tall adjacent walls.

```
+--------------------------------------------------------------+
| GPS Digital Address                                          |
| [ Signal Pulsing ] Live Satellite Accuracy: 6m (Good)        |
| Walk directly in front of the structure, then tap Lock GPS.  |
|                                                              |
|          [  🔒 LOCK GPS POSITION  ]                          |
|                                                              |
| [ Average Readings (5 Fixes) ] [ Adjust on Interactive Map ] |
+--------------------------------------------------------------+
```

### Step 4.1: Background Satellite Warmup
As soon as you open the form, the app begins connecting to GPS satellites in the background. Look at the status banner:
* 🔵 *"Acquiring satellite signal in background..."* — Satellites are connecting.
* 🟢 *"Live Satellite Accuracy: 6m (Good)"* — High quality satellite fix detected!

### Step 4.2: Tapping "Lock GPS Position"
1. Stand directly in front of the property.
2. When the accuracy shows **15m or better** (e.g. 4m, 6m, 10m), tap the green button:  
   👉 **`[ 🔒 Lock GPS Position ]`**
3. The button will change to **"Re-capture GPS Fix"**, and a green badge will appear: **"GPS Locked"**.
4. **The Photo Section below will now instantly unlock!**

---

### What to Do if Accuracy is Poor (Above 15 meters)

#### Method A: Use "Average Readings (5 Fixes)"
If you are between buildings where the signal fluctuates:
1. Tap the button: **`[ Average Readings (5 Fixes) ]`**.
2. Hold your phone steady for 4 seconds while the app takes 5 consecutive fixes.
3. The system calculates a weighted mathematical average and locks the best position.

#### Method B: Use "Adjust Pin on Interactive Map" (With Coordinate Jump)
If device GPS is jumping or obstructed:
1. Tap **"Place or adjust pin on interactive map"**.
2. A satellite map of Ga South will open.
3. **If you know the exact coordinates**: Type or paste them into the **Latitude** and **Longitude** boxes at the top and tap **"Jump Pin"**. The map will smoothly fly to that spot!
4. **Drag the Pin**: You can also touch and drag the red pin directly onto the building roof or front gate.
5. Tap **`[ Confirm Pin ]`** at the bottom.

#### What if I tapped "Lock GPS Position" by mistake?
Don't worry! Simply tap **`[ 🔄 Re-capture GPS Fix ]`** or tap the map pin option. It will immediately reset and let you capture the right spot.

The Assembly requires verifiable photographic evidence for every property or business on the register.

```
+---------------------------------------------------------------+
| Property / Signboard Photographs        [ Auto-Geotag Active ]|
|                                                               |
|  +--------------------+   +--------------------+              |
|  | [📷 Take Photo]    |   | [🖼️ From Gallery]  |              |
|  +--------------------+   +--------------------+              |
|  Each photo is permanently stamped with official GSMA GPS     |
|  coordinates, date, time, and collector identity.             |
+---------------------------------------------------------------+
```

### Step 5.1: Unlocking the Camera
* **Notice**: If you have not locked your GPS yet, the camera buttons are grayed out and show **"Camera Locked — Capture GPS first"**.
* Once GPS is locked, the camera unlocks with a green badge: **"Auto-Geotag Active"**.

### Step 5.2: Taking the Photos
Tap the camera icon to snap up to 4 photos per property:
1. **Photo 1 (Front Facade)**: Stand back and take a full shot of the entire building or business front.
2. **Photo 2 (Signboard / Business Name)**: Clear shot of the business signboard, banner, or store name.
3. **Photo 3 (House Number / Digital Address Plate)**: If the property has a painted house number or Ghana Post metal plate on the wall.
4. **Photo 4 (Additional Angle / Adjoining Street)**.

### Step 5.3: The Official Verification Watermark
When you take a photo, the app automatically burns an official evidentiary watermark across the bottom of the picture:
```
========================================================================
GA SOUTH MUNICIPAL ASSEMBLY — OFFICIAL VERIFICATION
Lat: 5.534210  Lng: -0.428512  Acc: 6m  | Loc: Galilea Market Road
Officer: Ernest Boateng-Gyasi  | Date: 12-Nov-2026 10:14 GMT
========================================================================
```
*This watermark cannot be forged or removed, providing 100% audit protection for your field work.*

## Module 6: Filling Ratepayer Details & Submitting Your Work

After locking GPS and taking photos, review and verify the ratepayer details on the form:

### 6.1 Key Fields to Verify:
1. **Location / Suburb** (*Mandatory*): e.g. `Amanfrom Central`, `Tuba`, `Galilea`, `Broadcasting`.
2. **Ghana Post Address** (*Optional*): e.g. `GS-0124-5678`.
3. **Owner / Ratepayer Name**: Full name of the property owner or business manager.
4. **Primary Phone Number**: Current active mobile number for SMS bills and payment notices.
5. **Structural / Business Specifics**:
   * For **Property**: Construction type (Single storey, Multi-storey block, Container, etc.), number of floors, occupancy.
   * For **BOP**: Business registration status, category, trade description.
   * For **Market Stall**: Shed/Store number, commodity type, toll rate.

### 6.2 Saving as Draft vs. Submitting:
* **Save as Draft**: Tap this if the owner is not around or you need to return later to get their phone number. It will save all coordinates and photos without sending for final billing.
* **Submit Record**: Tap this when all details, GPS, and photos are complete! The record changes to **Submitted** and moves to your supervisor's queue for final sign-off.

## Module 7: Supervisor Guide (Review, Approvals & Route Tracking)

Supervisors and Zonal Heads are responsible for quality control and field integrity.

### 7.1 Reviewing & Approving Records
1. Go to **Records** $\rightarrow$ filter by status: **"Submitted"**.
2. Click any record to review the collector's work:
   * Inspect the locked coordinates on the mini-map.
   * Click the photo thumbnail to view the full image and verify the watermark.
   * Check ratepayer phone number and property classification.
3. **Approve**: Click **`[ Approve Record ]`** to finalize into the billing ledger.
4. **Reject / Request Resurvey**: Click **`[ Reject ]`**, type a reason (e.g., *"Photo is blurry, please retake building front"*). The record goes back to the collector as a draft to correct.

### 7.2 Using "Trace Officer Route" in Map Explorer
1. Open the **Map Explorer** (`/map`).
2. Tap the button: **`[ Trace Officer Route ]`**.
3. Select the **Field Officer** and pick the **Date** (e.g., today).
4. Tap **`[ Trace Field Route ]`**:
   * The map will draw a purple path connecting every property the officer visited that day in order.
   * Check the stats: **Total Records**, **Distance Walked (km)**, and **Active Hours**.
   * Pin #1 (Green) is where they started in the morning; the Red Pin is where they finished.
   * Tap any number along the line to inspect the property visited and the exact time visited!

## Module 8: Field Troubleshooting & Common Questions (FAQ)

#### Q1: "My phone says 'Location permission denied' when I try to capture GPS. What should I do?"
* **Android**: Open Chrome $\rightarrow$ tap 3 dots (top right) $\rightarrow$ Settings $\rightarrow$ Site Settings $\rightarrow$ Location $\rightarrow$ find `gasouth-data-app.onrender.com` $\rightarrow$ tap **"Allow"**.
* **iPhone**: Open Settings $\rightarrow$ Safari $\rightarrow$ Location $\rightarrow$ change to **"Ask"** or **"Allow"**. Then refresh the page.

#### Q2: "The camera buttons are locked and grayed out. Why?"
* The camera is locked because you haven't captured GPS coordinates yet. Stand outside in front of the building and tap **"Lock GPS Position"**. As soon as GPS is locked, the camera unlocks immediately!

#### Q3: "What if there is poor internet connectivity in my zone?"
* The DCS has built-in offline autosave! As you type, the form constantly saves your work into your phone's memory. Even if your browser reloads or your connection drops briefly, your entered text and photos will still be there. When connectivity returns, tap **"Submit Record"**.

#### Q4: "I accidentally locked the GPS while walking down the street before reaching the shop. How do I fix it?"
* Simply tap the **`[ 🔄 Re-capture GPS Fix ]`** button once you arrive directly in front of the shop. It will instantly erase the old reading and lock your current exact position.

#### Q5: "Who do I contact if I forget my password or get locked out?"
* Contact the Ga South Municipal IT Unit or your assigned Zonal Supervisor. They can reset your password immediately in the User Management console without losing any of your saved records.
